Frequently Asked Questions

Where do deleted photos go?
Nothing is deleted when you swipe. Deletions happen only on the Review screen, after you confirm the standard iOS prompt. Deleted photos then sit in the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for about 30 days, where iOS lets you recover them.

Why does Rollcall ask for access to my whole photo library?
Sorting your camera roll means seeing all of it. With the "limited access" option, iOS would only show Rollcall the photos you had already picked by hand, which defeats the point of sorting. Rollcall reads your library through Apple's Photos framework and nothing else: no uploading, no copying, no analysis off your phone.

Does anything leave my phone?
No. Rollcall has no server, no account, and makes no network connections. Your photos, your decisions, and your settings stay on your device.

What is the free daily limit?
You get 15 free swipes every day, forever. The counter resets at midnight, local time. Rollcall Pro removes the limit.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, and cancel there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription (that is true of every iOS app).

I paid on my old phone. How do I get Pro back?
Open the paywall or Settings in Rollcall and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription or lifetime unlock is tied to your Apple account.

What happens to my progress if I stop paying?
Nothing. You keep every decision and go back to 15 free swipes a day.
